Kiroq Privacy Policy

Developer: Toshihiro.nakao
Effective date: October 24, 2026

Toshihiro.nakao ("we", "us") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how the iOS / iPadOS app "Kiroq" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Our Approach

The App keeps a restaurant's food hygiene records on your device and in your own iCloud. We do not collect any of your information.

  • The App does not send information to our servers. We do not operate any servers.
  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • We do not show ads, analyze usage, or track you.
  • The App does not include any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

The settings screen includes a link to a hygiene management guide on an external website. That website's own privacy policy applies once you open it.

2. Information the App Handles

The App stores the following information that you enter:

  • Store details (store name, business type, the time the business day changes, regular closing days, reminder times, and so on)
  • Your hygiene management plan (checklist items, critical control points, refrigerators, freezers and other equipment, and their temperature limits)
  • Daily records (pass/fail results, temperatures, notes, who recorded each entry, the stamps used to close each sheet, revision history, and days marked as closed)
  • Weekly reviews and monthly reflections (who reviewed, the review stamp or a handwritten signature, and the reflection text)
  • The names, roles and recording-screen languages of the staff who keep the records

Handling staff names

The App lets you register the names of staff who work at your store so the records show who recorded or reviewed each entry. You can also import names from an employee list (CSV file) exported from an attendance or shift app; the import happens on your device. When you register staff names, please keep to what the store's records actually need, and let the people concerned know where appropriate.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device: Records are stored in the App's own storage. For the Home Screen widget, the App also saves a summary of today's records (such as the store name and the number of remaining items) in a shared area on your device.
  • iCloud: If your device is signed in to iCloud, your records are stored in your own private iCloud storage and sync across devices that use the same Apple ID. We cannot see this data.
  • Sharing with staff: When you share a store, its records are shared through iCloud sharing with the people you invite. They can view the store's records and, if you allow it, add records. You can stop sharing at any time, which removes the store from their devices.
  • Exports: When you create a PDF, CSV or backup (JSON) file, it is handed to the location or recipient you choose. How it is handled from there depends on that service.
  • Deleting the App: Deleting the App removes the records stored on your device. Records saved in iCloud remain and come back if you reinstall the App.

4. Access to Device Features

  • Camera: Used to read the display of a thermometer and put the value into the temperature field. No photos are saved. If you do not allow access, you can still enter temperatures with the keypad.
  • NFC: Used to read tags attached to your equipment and open that equipment's temperature entry. The App only writes a link that opens the equipment (including its identifier) to a tag. If you do not allow access, all other features remain available.
  • Notifications: Used for the before-opening reminder, the after-closing reminder for unstamped sheets, and reminders for weekly reviews, monthly reflections and plan reviews. Notifications are scheduled on your device. If you do not allow notifications, you can still keep records.
  • Widgets and the Lock Screen: Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and the Lock Screen display (Live Activity), show the store name and today's progress. You can turn these off in the App's settings or your device settings.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: "Record temperature" and "Open today's sheet" are available from Siri and Shortcuts. Voice recognition is handled by Apple's features; the App only receives the equipment you chose and the temperature.

5. On-Device Processing

  • Reading a thermometer's display (text recognition) happens on your device.
  • On supported devices, drafts of corrective notes and monthly reflections are written by a language model that runs on your device. Your records are not sent to us or to anyone else.

6. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by the App Store. We never receive your payment details, such as credit card information. The App receives your purchase records from the App Store only to check what you have purchased.

7. Sharing With Third Parties

Because we do not collect your information, we do not share it with third parties. Anything passed to the people you share a store with, or to the destinations of files you export, is passed by your own action.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • To keep the records trustworthy, individual daily records cannot be deleted one by one.
  • "Delete this store" in the settings deletes that store's records from your device and from iCloud. For a store you joined through sharing, "Leave sharing" removes the store from your device; the owner's records remain.
  • You can also delete data stored in iCloud from your device's Settings, under iCloud storage management.

9. Children

The App is designed for restaurant operations and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes take effect when the updated policy is posted on this page.

11. Information We Receive When You Contact Us

If you contact us through the support page, we receive your message and the contact details you provide for our reply, such as your email address. We use them only to respond to your inquiry, do not keep them longer than necessary once it is resolved, and do not share them with third parties. Inquiries are received through Google Forms (Google LLC), and Google LLC's privacy policy also applies when you submit the form.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy, please contact us through the support page.
