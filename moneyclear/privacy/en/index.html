Privacy Policy (MoneyClear)

Developer: Toshihiro.nakao
Effective date: October 24, 2026

Toshihiro.nakao ("we", "us") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how the iOS / iPadOS app "MoneyClear" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Our Approach

The App keeps the budget and asset information you enter only on your device and in your own iCloud. We never receive this information.

  • The App does not send information to any server we operate.
  • There is no account registration or sign-in.
  • We do not show ads, analyze usage, or track you.
  • The App uses no third-party libraries (SDKs) other than frameworks provided by Apple.

2. Information the App Handles

The App stores the following information that you enter:

  • Accounts (name, icon, starting balance, expected return, and volatility)
  • Entries (year and month, type — income, expense, savings, or investment — description, amount, account used, and repeat settings)
  • Forecast assumptions (current age, retirement age, life expectancy, withdrawal method, and which accounts to include)
  • Display settings (such as dark mode and button color) and a log of sync status

The App has no fields for your name, contact details, bank or brokerage login credentials, or account numbers, and it never connects to financial institutions to retrieve balances or transactions.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device: The information you enter is stored on your device.
  • iCloud: Only if you have purchased the Sync plan and are signed in to iCloud on your device, the information you enter is also stored in the private area of your own iCloud through Apple's CloudKit, and synced across devices signed in with the same Apple Account. Only you can access this area; we cannot see its contents. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to how iCloud handles your data.
  • Backup export: Using "Export backup" in Settings saves your records as a JSON file to a location you choose. You manage the exported file, and it is never sent to us.

Deleting the App removes the data stored on your device. Data stored in iCloud remains after you delete the App (see "6. Deleting Your Data" for how to remove it).

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers one-time in-app purchases that unlock paid features. Purchases and payments are processed by Apple's App Store, and we never receive payment information such as credit card numbers. The App only receives your purchase records from the App Store to check whether a feature has been purchased.

5. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not share your information with third parties, except where disclosure is required by law.

6. Deleting Your Data

  • "Delete all data" in Settings removes your entries and accounts from this device. If you sync with the Sync plan, it also removes them from every device synced with the same iCloud. This cannot be undone.
  • "Remove sample data" in Settings removes only the accounts and entries that were added as sample data.
  • You can also delete the App's data stored in iCloud from iCloud storage management in the Settings app on your device.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as needed. The updated Policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.

9. Information We Receive When You Contact Us

Inquiries through the support page are received using Google Forms (Google LLC). The information you enter when contacting us (such as your name, email address, and message) is used only to respond to you and to improve the App, and is never shared with third parties. We delete it once it is no longer needed after we respond. Google's Privacy Policy also applies when you submit an inquiry.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy, please contact us through the support page.
