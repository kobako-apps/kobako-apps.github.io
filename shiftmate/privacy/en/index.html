Privacy Policy (Shiftmate)

Effective date: June 13, 2026 / Last updated: October 22, 2026
Developer: Toshihiro.nakao

Toshihiro.nakao ("we", "us") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how the iOS / iPadOS app "Shiftmate" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Our Approach

  • Your data is stored on your device. We cannot read the data you enter in the App.
  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • We do not show ads, analyze usage or track you, and the App contains no third-party SDKs for those purposes.
  • Only if you use the optional "Shiftmate Link" subscription, content encrypted in the App and in your staff's browsers is kept temporarily on a server we operate. We do not hold the keys to it and cannot read it (Section 6).

2. Information the App Handles

You can enter and create the following in the App:

  • Business details (name, the shop's time zone, staffing needs and other settings)
  • Staff details (name, color, hourly wage and scheduled wage changes, roles and duties, availability such as days and times they can't work, temporary conflicts and weekly limits, phone number, notes, last working day)
  • Shifts (scheduled shifts, shift requests, day-off requests, swap requests, and records of call-outs and cover)
  • Manager and staff PINs (never stored in plain text; only a SHA-256 hash is kept)
  • Requests your staff send from their "Shiftmate Link" pages (their choice for each day)

Personal information about your staff

If you enter your staff's names, contact details or other personal information, you, as the business, are responsible for collecting, using and managing it lawfully. When you use "Shiftmate Link", staff names and shifts are kept, encrypted, on our server, so please let your staff know before you share or collect by link.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • On your device: The App's data is stored in the App's own area on your device. Summaries shown in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets (today's crew, shortages, number of to-dos, etc.) are also stored on the device, in an area only the App and its widgets can read.
  • On-device search index: So you can find shifts from your device's search (Spotlight) or Siri, the App adds what the schedule shows (staff name, business name, date, start and end time, status) to a search index managed by iOS on your device. Wages, labor cost, phone numbers and notes are not added.
  • iCloud (if you have bought Premium and are signed in to iCloud): For sync and backup across your devices, your data is stored in your own iCloud account (CloudKit private database). Only you can read it; we cannot.
  • iCloud Keychain (if you use "Shiftmate Link"): The keys that open staff pages and the secret values used to update them are stored in your device's keychain and, if you use iCloud Keychain, synced to your other devices.
  • Our server (only if you use "Shiftmate Link"): Content you share or collect by link is kept there temporarily, encrypted (Section 6).

Deleting the App removes its data from your device. Data in iCloud is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Access to Device Features

The App asks for the following permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. Granting them is optional, every other feature works without them, and you can change them at any time in the Settings app.

  • Calendar (write only): Used to add confirmed shifts to your device's calendar. The App never reads your calendar.
  • Notifications: Used for the day-before shift reminder (a local notification on your device) and, with "Shiftmate Link", to tell you when requests arrive and when a deadline is near. Request notifications arrive as Apple push notifications, and staff names are filled in on your device.
  • Biometrics (Face ID / Touch ID): Used only if you turn on biometric unlock for the manager screens. iOS performs the authentication; the App never receives biometric data.

Also, only when you tap to contact a staff member, the App opens your device's Phone or Messages app with the phone number you entered. The App does not send the number anywhere.

5. On-Device Processing

  • On supported devices running iOS 27 or later, what you type in plain words in the To Do chat is read by the on-device language model (Apple Intelligence) to propose changes. Your text is processed on the device and never sent to us. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.
  • Finding and reading out shifts from your device's search, Siri and Shortcuts uses the on-device index (Section 3).

6. Third-Party Services

What "Shiftmate Link" keeps on our server

The following applies only if you use the optional "Shiftmate Link" subscription. If you don't, none of this information is sent.

  • What is sent: For a schedule link, the business name, staff names and colors, and the dates, times and breaks of confirmed shifts and days off. For a request link, the business name, the person's name, the period, the names and times of shift templates, and whether each day has plans plus the current request. For each staff member's page, the business name, the person's name and color, and the keys that open the two links above. Requests your staff send (their choice for each day). Wages, labor cost, phone numbers, notes, PINs and availability are never sent.
  • Encryption: All of this is encrypted with AES-256-GCM in the App or in the staff member's browser before it is sent. The key is only in the part of the link after "#", which browsers never send to the server, so neither we nor the hosting company can read the content. Anyone who has a link can read its content, so share links only with the people they are meant for.
  • Where it is kept: A server we operate on Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) services (Cloudflare Workers and D1). Servers may be located outside your country.
  • Also stored: The link ID, creation and update times, a time showing how current the content is, the expiry, the deadline, a secret value that authorizes updates (as a hash only), a random value identifying where to send notifications, and a value used to verify those notifications.
  • How long it is kept: Schedule links and staff pages are deleted 35 days after their last update; request links 7 days after the deadline (or 31 days after the last update if there is no deadline). Staff requests are deleted from the server as soon as the App receives them. Content is also deleted when you stop a link, delete a business, or use "Delete All Data".
  • Server logs: IP addresses are used in the moment to limit repeated requests and are not stored. Server logs never contain the content sent, IP addresses or headers.
  • Purchase verification: The server also checks your subscription using the purchase record signed by Apple. The record is only checked, never stored.
  • Staff browsers: A staff page stores the chosen name and sent requests only in that browser (they can be cleared in the browser's settings).

Apple services

  • CloudKit public database (when collecting requests with "Shiftmate Link"): To notify your device that requests have arrived, the App places an "arrived" marker containing only a meaningless random value and a time. It is deleted after 7 days.
  • App Store public information: To check for a new version, the App queries Apple's lookup API at most once a day, sending only the App's ID and your country.

Apple services are provided under Apple's Privacy Policy and terms.

7. In-App Purchases

Premium is a one-time (non-consumable) purchase, and "Shiftmate Link" is an auto-renewable subscription. Apple (the App Store) handles all purchasing, billing and cancellation; we never receive your credit card or other payment details. The App only learns whether you have purchased. For "Shiftmate Link", the server receives the purchase record signed by Apple to confirm it is active, and does not store it.

8. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not share or sell your data to third parties, except where required by law. The only thing sent to our server is the encrypted content of "Shiftmate Link", if you use it (Section 6).

9. Deleting Your Data

  • You can delete data with "Delete All Data" in the App's settings, or by deleting staff or businesses. The on-device search index is updated at the same time.
  • Content kept on our server for "Shiftmate Link" is deleted after the periods in Section 6, and also when you stop a link, delete a business, or delete all data.
  • Deleting the App removes its data from your device. You can manage and delete data in iCloud from your device's iCloud settings.
  • To keep shifts out of your device's search and Siri, turn off Siri (Siri & Search) for the App in iOS Settings.

10. Children

The App is for businesses managing their staff's shifts and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as laws change or features are added. The updated policy takes effect when it is posted.

12. Information We Receive When You Contact Us

The contact form on our support page uses Google Forms, provided by Google LLC. Information you enter when contacting us (such as your name, reply email address and message) is used only to answer you and to investigate and improve issues, and is not shared with third parties except where required by law. We delete it once it is no longer needed. Google's Privacy Policy also applies to the form. Using the App never sends this information to us.

13. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy, please contact us through the support page.
