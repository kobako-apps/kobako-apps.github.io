The work schedule, faster than paper.

Still building the schedule on paper, a whiteboard or a spreadsheet? Tap AM or PM and the week fills in, and short-staffed days and hours stand out at a glance. Then send it to staff with one link, even if they don't have the app.

Built for managers who make the schedule themselves

Cafes, restaurants, salons, retail stores, studios and tutoring schools with a handful to about 20 staff.

A paper-style grid you fill with taps

Tap a template to add a shift to the staff-by-day grid, and keep going day by day while you watch the week fill in.

  • Copy last week, and confirm draft shifts all at once
  • Switch between Day, Week and Month, and swipe to the next week or day
  • Set regular closed days by weekday

See short-staffed hours, and where to add

Set staffing needs by weekday, by time of day (for example, 3 people from 11a to 2p every day) and by role. The Day view lists what's missing, and "Add Here" suggests the shift that covers the most missing hours, and who is available to work it.

  • Press and hold a shift bar and drag its edges to change the time
  • Alerts for hours with no one on shift, long runs of days in a row, short breaks and short rest between shifts

Headcount by hour, as a heatmap

The week view has a table and a heatmap. The heatmap shades every hour of the week by how many people are working, so a thin lunch rush is easy to spot.

Collect staff requests by a deadline

Staff send shift requests, day-off requests and swap requests from their Shiftmate Link page, or from Staff View on the shop's device. Weekly Requests let them send a whole week at once, and late requests are marked.

  • To Do puts the most urgent shortage first, and you approve or decline requests right there
  • Staff View has a personal PIN for each person, so no one can change someone else's shifts
New

Call-outs and swaps,
covered on the spot

Mark a shift as a call-out and see who can step in, with overtime and days-in-a-row notes, then give them the shift. You can also hand off the rest of a shift partway through.

  • For a swap, see who can take it and the effect on hours, roles and labor cost before you reassign
  • People with an overlapping shift, a day off or a day-off request never show up as options, including shifts at your other locations
New

The app remembers
each person's limits

"Only Saturday afternoons." "Still training in the kitchen." You don't have to keep it all in your head. Save each person's availability and roles, and the app flags mismatches as you schedule (nothing is blocked).

  • Days and times they can't work, temporary conflicts, weekly limits and preferred shift length
  • Several roles per person (front, kitchen), with a trainee mark
  • Alerts when no one can open, close or lead
Premium

Fill gaps automatically

Creates draft shifts for short days from your staffing needs and staff requests. It respects each person's availability and roles, rest between shifts and fairness across the team. Nothing is final until you review and confirm.

Post it, share it, add it to calendars

Add confirmed shifts to the calendar on your device. Share the schedule as an image or PDF (Letter in the U.S.), print it for the back room, or send a calendar file staff can add to their own calendar. Export totals as CSV by pay period.

  • Image, PDF, calendar file (.ics) and CSV export are Premium

Reach staff without the app,
with one link

Stop re-sending photos of the schedule. The same link always shows the latest version.

Shiftmate Link

A page for each person,
with their shifts and everyone's

Send each staff member a link to their own page. In any browser, no app needed, they see their next shift, their own shifts and everyone's. When you change a shift, the same link shows the update, so there's nothing to send again.

  • Open requests for a period with a deadline. Staff send them from their page, and they land in To Do for approval, with a notification
  • See who has sent requests and who hasn't, and remind the rest
  • Turn the shared schedule into a QR code to post in the back room
  • Getting Started in 3 Steps walks you through creating pages, opening requests and sending links

Even we can't read it

Everything is encrypted on the shop's device and in your staff's browsers before it's sent. The key exists only inside the link.

Only confirmed shifts and days off

Hourly wages, labor cost, phone numbers and notes are never included. Draft shifts and other people's requests aren't either.

Replace a link anytime

Don't want someone who left to see it? Tap Replace Link. Deactivating a staff member also turns off their page.

Shiftmate Link is a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, separate from Premium.

For the rest of the day, too

Once the schedule is set, today's crew and the pay period's totals live in the app as well.

Today's Staffing at a glance

Headcount through the day and who's on now. Call, message or adjust someone right from their row. Tomorrow's shortages show up here and in the day-before reminder.

Labor cost and hours

Hours and estimated labor cost per person, with U.S. federal (FLSA) overtime or a custom rule, totaled by your pay period. Book a raise ahead of time; overnight shifts split the rate at midnight.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

See today's staffing, shortages and to-dos without opening the app, and get an optional reminder the day before each workday.

Search and voice

Find shifts by staff name from your device's search, or ask for today's or tomorrow's shifts by voice.

Type requests in plain words

Type "Emma is off next Tuesday" and get proposed changes. Nothing is saved until you tap Apply.
iOS 27 on supported devices. Everything stays on your device.

Admin PIN lock

Labor costs and staff details stay behind an admin PIN, with biometric unlock supported. Choose a light or dark appearance.

Pay figures are estimates of gross pay. Taxes and deductions are not included.

Your data stays on your device

No sign-up. Your data is stored on your device and works without a connection (except sending and receiving Shiftmate Link). What you share by link is encrypted, so even we can't read it. There's no analytics or advertising inside.
