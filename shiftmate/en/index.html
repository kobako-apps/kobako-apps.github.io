Built for managers who make the schedule themselves

Cafes, restaurants, salons, retail stores, studios and tutoring schools with a handful to about 20 staff.

A paper-style grid you fill with taps

Tap a template to add a shift to the staff-by-day grid, and keep going day by day while you watch the week fill in.

  • Copy last week, and confirm draft shifts all at once
  • Switch between Day, Week and Month
  • Set regular closed days by weekday

See short-staffed hours, and where to add

Set staffing needs by weekday and by time of day (for example, 3 people from 11a to 2p every day). In the Day view, "Add Here" suggests the shift that covers the most missing hours, and who is available to work it.

  • Press and hold a shift bar and drag its edges to change the time
  • Alerts for hours with no one on shift, long runs of days in a row, short breaks and short rest between shifts

Headcount by hour, as a heatmap

The week view has a table and a heatmap. The heatmap shades every hour of the week by how many people are working, so a thin lunch rush is easy to spot.

Collect staff requests by a deadline

On the shop's device, staff open Staff View to check their own shifts and send shift requests, day-off requests and swap requests. Weekly Requests let them send a whole week at once, and late requests are marked.

  • Approve or decline everything from the To Do tab
  • Each staff member has a personal PIN, so no one can change someone else's shifts
Premium

Fill gaps automatically

Creates draft shifts for short days from your staffing needs and staff requests. It respects weekly hour limits, days each person can't work, rest between shifts and fairness across the team. Nothing is final until you review and confirm.

Post it, share it, add it to calendars

Add confirmed shifts to the calendar on your device. Share the schedule as an image or PDF, print it for the back room, or send a calendar file staff can add to their own calendar.

  • Image, PDF, calendar file (.ics) and CSV export are Premium

For the rest of the day, too

Once the schedule is set, today's crew and the pay period's totals live in the app as well.

Today at a glance

Who's working, when, and today's labor cost. Tomorrow's shortages show up here too.

Labor cost and hours

Hours and estimated labor cost per person, with U.S. federal (FLSA) overtime or a custom rule, totaled by your pay period.

Home Screen widget

See today's crew without opening the app, and get an optional reminder the day before each workday.

Search and voice

Find shifts by staff name from your device's search, or ask for today's or tomorrow's shifts by voice.

Type requests in plain words

Type "Emma is off next Tuesday" and get proposed changes. Nothing is saved until you tap Apply.
iOS 27 on supported devices. Everything stays on your device.

Admin PIN lock

Labor costs and staff details stay behind an admin PIN, with biometric unlock supported.

Pay figures are estimates of gross pay. Taxes and deductions are not included.

Your data stays on your device

No sign-up. Your data is stored on your device and works without a connection. The developer never collects it, and there's no analytics or advertising inside.
