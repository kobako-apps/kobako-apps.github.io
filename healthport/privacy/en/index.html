HealthPort Privacy Policy

Developer: Toshihiro.nakao
Effective date: November 12, 2026

Toshihiro.nakao ("we", "us") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how the iOS app "HealthPort" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Our Approach

The App reads health data recorded in Apple's Health app and turns it into PDF and CSV reports you can bring to a doctor's appointment. All processing happens on your device, and we do not collect any of your information.

  • Your health data and notes are never sent to our servers or anywhere else.
  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • The App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • The App does not use any third-party libraries (SDKs).

2. Information the App Handles

The App handles the following information on your device:

  • Health data: the categories you allow (such as blood pressure, heart rate, weight, steps, sleep, body temperature, blood glucose, and medications). The App reads them to create reports and display them on screen.
  • Blood pressure and pulse you enter: values you enter in the App are saved to the Health app.
  • Condition and medication notes: the date, free-text notes, condition tags (such as headache or dizziness), and whether you took your medication.
  • Settings: the name shown on reports, blood pressure targets, the categories you choose to show, reminder times, appearance, and similar preferences.
  • Reports you create: the PDF and CSV files you create and their history.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Your notes, settings, and reports are stored only on your device, in the App's private storage.
  • Blood pressure and pulse you enter are stored in the Health app on your device and are managed according to your Health app settings.
  • The App does not sync through iCloud. However, if you back up your device (to iCloud or a computer), the App's data may be included in that backup.
  • If you send or save a report using the share sheet, the destination app or service you choose handles it under its own policies.
  • Deleting the App removes its data stored on your device (notes, settings, and reports). Blood pressure and pulse saved to the Health app remain there even after the App is deleted.

4. Access to Device Features

Health

The App asks to read the Health data categories you allow so it can create and display reports, and asks for write access so it can save the blood pressure and pulse you enter. You can change access for each category at any time in the Health app's settings. Categories you do not allow are simply left out of reports.

Notifications

The App asks for permission to send notifications for morning and evening blood pressure reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device and do not send any information off it. All other features work without this permission.

5. On-Device Processing

Creating reports (PDF and CSV), including the "Create Blood Pressure Report" action available in the Shortcuts app, is done entirely on your device.

6. In-App Purchases

Purchases of the App's paid features (Pro) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, credit card number, or other payment details. The App only checks with the App Store whether you have made a purchase.

7. Sharing With Third Parties

Because we do not collect your information, we do not share it with any third party.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • You can delete condition and medication notes one at a time from the notes screen.
  • You can delete reports from the report history screen, either one at a time or in bulk with "Delete reports older than 30 days" or "Delete all reports".
  • Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.
  • Blood pressure and pulse saved to the Health app can be deleted from the Health app.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes take effect when the updated policy is posted on this page.

11. Information We Receive When You Contact Us

The contact form on the support page uses Google Forms, provided by Google LLC. When you contact us, we receive the information you enter (such as your reply email address and the content of your inquiry).

  • We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and to investigate and fix issues
  • We do not share it with any third party, except as required by law
  • We delete it once we have finished handling your inquiry and it is no longer needed
  • Google's privacy policy also applies to how the form handles your information

This applies only when you choose to send us an inquiry. Using the App never sends your information to us.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy, please contact us through the support page.
