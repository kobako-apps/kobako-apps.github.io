NextPay Privacy Policy

Developer: Toshihiro.nakao
Effective date: November 14, 2026

Toshihiro.nakao ("we", "us") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how the iOS / iPadOS app "NextPay" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Our Approach

The App does not collect your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device, and we cannot see or receive it.

  • We do not operate a server, and the App does not send your data or the contents of your pay slips over the network.
  • There is no account registration or sign-in.
  • The App does not show ads, analyze usage, collect crash reports, or track you.
  • The App does not include any third-party libraries (SDKs).

2. Information the App Handles

To provide its features, the App stores the following information that you enter, on your device only:

  • Names of salary items and allowances, and your monthly pay amounts
  • Deduction details imported from pay slips (income tax, resident tax, social insurance, and so on) and net pay
  • How pay slip item names map to the App's salary items
  • Bonus base amounts, payout rates, evaluation ranks, and bonus formulas
  • Which salary items are included when calculating your raise rate
  • Retirement allowance settings, formulas, and custom items
  • Settings used to estimate take-home pay (number of dependents, insurance rates, and so on)
  • Your starting age, planned retirement age, and display settings

Importing Pay Slip PDFs

The App can read pay slip and bonus statement PDFs that you choose and fill in the amounts.

  • The App only reads PDFs in the files or folders you select in the file picker.
  • The App does not keep the PDF files themselves. It only saves the amounts and item names you review and confirm with 「反映」 (Apply).

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Your data is stored in the App's own storage area on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, the App's data may be included in those backups as part of standard iOS behavior. Those backups are managed by Apple or by you, and we cannot access them.
  • The App's backup and restore feature (Pro) lets you export all of your data to a single file and restore from it. You choose where to save the file in the file picker. The exported file is not encrypted, so please be careful where you store or share it. If you save it to a cloud service, that service's privacy policy applies.
  • If you delete the App from your device, all data stored on that device is deleted.

4. Access to Device Features

The App does not request access to your camera, photos, location, microphone, contacts, or notifications. For PDF imports and backup files, the App only reads and writes the files you choose in the standard iOS file picker.

5. On-Device Processing

Pay slip PDFs are read entirely on your device.

  • For PDFs that contain only images and no text, the App uses the built-in iOS text recognition to read the text on your device.
  • On supported devices, the App may use Apple's on-device AI (Foundation Models) to read pay slip items. This model runs on your device, and the contents of your pay slips are never sent to Apple or to us.

6. In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time in-app purchase (NextPay Pro) that unlocks additional features. Purchases are processed by the App Store.

  • Your payment information, such as credit card details, is handled by Apple. We never receive it.
  • The App checks your purchase status with the App Store only to unlock the features you have purchased.
  • Apple's Privacy Policy applies to information handled during purchases.

7. Sharing With Third Parties

Because we do not hold your data, we never share or sell it to third parties. We also do not share information we receive when you contact us, except as required by law.

8. Deleting Your Data

  • You can delete a single year's data by pressing and holding the year's name on the 推移 (History) tab.
  • You can reset all data to its initial state with 「すべてのデータを削除」 (Delete All Data) in 設定 (Settings).
  • Deleting the App from your device deletes all of its data on that device.
  • Because we do not hold your data, we cannot recover data you have deleted.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. Regardless of age, the App does not collect personal information from anyone.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features change or when laws are revised. Any changes take effect when the updated policy is posted on this page.

11. Information We Receive When You Contact Us

Inquiries through our support page are handled with Google Forms (Google LLC). When you contact us, we receive the information you enter, such as your message and any email address you provide for our reply.

  • We use this information only to respond to you and to improve the App.
  • We delete the information once it is no longer needed after we have responded.
  • Google's Privacy Policy also applies when you submit an inquiry.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy, please contact us through the support page.
