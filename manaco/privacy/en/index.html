manaco Privacy Policy

Developer: Toshihiro.nakao
Effective date: November 7, 2026

Toshihiro.nakao ("we", "us") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how the iOS / iPadOS app "manaco" (the "App") handles your information.

1. Our Approach

The App helps you keep track of your belongings and supplies, and it keeps that information on your device. We do not send your information to our servers or collect it.

  • The App does not send data to any server we operate
  • No account or sign-in is required
  • There is no advertising, usage analytics, or tracking
  • The App does not include third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, or crash reporting

2. Information the App Handles

The App stores the following information that you enter or add:

  • Photos of your items
  • Item names, brands or makers, categories, genres, storage locations, and notes
  • Stock counts, counting units, package counts and items per package, content amounts, low-stock thresholds, and prices
  • Deadline dates and times, and their notification and display settings
  • Latitude and longitude, if you choose to save a location
  • Shopping list entries and your home screen tabs (filters and sort order)
  • App settings, such as display preferences

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

This information is stored on your device.

  • iCloud Sync: Only if you purchase the "iCloud Sync" in-app purchase, your data is stored in the private area of your own iCloud account (CloudKit) and synced across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple manages this area, and we cannot see its contents.
  • Widgets: To show your Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the App writes the names, counts, and small thumbnails of low-stock items, along with deadline dates, to a shared area on your device that only the App and its widgets use. This information does not leave your device.
  • Backups and exports: When you create a backup or export CSV or JSON from Settings, the file goes to the destination you choose, such as the Files app, iCloud Drive, or another app. You decide where it is saved or sent.

Deleting the App from your device also deletes the data stored on that device. If you use iCloud Sync, the data in iCloud remains (see "9. Deleting Your Data").

4. Access to Device Features

Location

The App gets your current location only when you tap "Use Current Location" on an item's edit screen, and only while you are using the App. It saves the latitude and longitude with that item, and you can open the saved place in the Maps app from the item's detail screen. If you do not allow access, every feature other than saving a location still works.

Photos and files

The App only reads the photos you pick from your photo library or the Files app. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library.

Notifications

For items with deadline notifications turned on, the App schedules a notification on your device for the deadline. Notifications are scheduled on your device only, and our servers are not involved. If you do not allow notifications, every other feature still works.

5. On-Device Processing

  • White background: Cutting out the subject of a photo and placing it on a white background is done entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Your photos are not sent anywhere.
  • Shortcuts and Siri: So you can adjust stock from the Shortcuts app or Siri, the App provides item names and stock counts to iOS. They are handled through iOS features and are not sent to us.

6. Third-Party Services

When you save a location and the storage location field is empty, the App sends the latitude and longitude to Apple's location service (reverse geocoding) to suggest a place name. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to this processing. We do not receive this information.

7. In-App Purchases

The App's in-app purchases (Premium and iCloud Sync, both one-time purchases) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive payment details such as credit card numbers. The App only receives confirmation from the App Store of what you have purchased, and uses it to unlock features.

8. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not share your information with third parties, except as required by law. For third-party services, see "6. Third-Party Services".

9. Deleting Your Data

  • You can delete items at any time from the edit screen or quick edit, or by selecting several on the home screen.
  • "Delete all data" in Settings removes all items, tabs, and shopping list entries at once. If you use iCloud Sync, they are also removed from your other synced devices.
  • Deleting the App from your device also deletes the data on that device.
  • Data remaining in iCloud can be deleted from iCloud storage management in the Settings app on your device.

10. Children

The App is not directed primarily at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. An updated policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.

12. Information We Receive When You Contact Us

Inquiries through the support page are received using Google Forms (Google LLC). What you enter when you contact us, such as your name, email address, and message, is used only to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties. Once we have responded, we delete what we no longer need. Google's Privacy Policy also applies when you submit an inquiry.

13. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy, please contact us through the support page.
